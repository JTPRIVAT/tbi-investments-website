Area 02 · Algorithms & AI

Data and rules.
Applied consistently.

Algorithmic trading is based on criteria defined in advance. Market data, conditions and risk limits form the basis of a rule-based approach. Such a system follows its instructions without reacting spontaneously to fear, euphoria or other emotions at the moment of a decision.

THE UNDERLYING PRINCIPLE

Discipline and transparency

Clear criteria.
Consistent standards.

A rule-based approach specifies which information matters and under which conditions a response is intended. This prepares the decision before a particular market situation arises. The same criteria can be applied to many observations and reviewed afterwards.

This structure supports an objective comparison of assumptions and results. Changes can be explained, documented and tested again. Data quality, risk limits and technical oversight remain essential; applying a rule consistently does not by itself establish its economic viability.

Method

Facts, data
and discipline.

01

Reliable data

Source, timeliness and completeness determine what an analysis can establish. Carefully checked market data provides a basis for investigating observed relationships and challenging assumptions.

02

Explicit rules

Criteria for signals, responses and risk limits are defined in advance. This makes an approach testable and, when followed consistently, reduces the scope for spontaneous, emotionally driven intervention.

03

Systematic testing

A model is assessed against its assumptions and results. Historical data, costs and different market conditions form part of the evaluation before conclusions are drawn about an approach’s viability.

Artificial intelligence

Connect information.
Accelerate analysis.

We use artificial intelligence in our internal workflows. It helps us combine information from multiple sources, organise material and prepare analyses. We use it to process substantial amounts of information more quickly and focus our work on the questions that matter.

Developing new strategy ideas is another area of use. AI helps compare approaches, refine questions and prepare initial drafts. Source checks and professional assessment accompany this process; strategy ideas are then investigated separately.
