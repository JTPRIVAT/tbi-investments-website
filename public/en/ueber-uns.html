The project

Rooted in Switzerland.
Looking ahead.

The formation of TBI Investment und Trading GmbH is being prepared. This website, presented under the TBI Investments brand, introduces selected technology topics and perspectives.

Its focus is on how digital systems work, the development of rule-based models and the possibilities of artificial intelligence. No financial services are offered and no investment orders are accepted through this website.

The perspective

Markets and technology.
Connected through method.

Digital markets call for economic understanding and a precise approach to data. Analysis of price movements, liquidity and market conditions complements the technical work on rule-based models. Both perspectives help turn an idea into a specific question for investigation.

We use AI internally to organise information, support analyses and develop new strategy approaches. It supports a structured workflow in which sources are compared, assumptions are formulated and results are assessed in context.

Principles

Analyse clearly.
Develop ideas with discipline.

01

Analytical clarity

Observations, assumptions and conclusions are kept distinct. Clear questions and traceable data make an analysis comparable and open to scrutiny.

02

Practical technology

New technologies are assessed by their contribution to the work: organising information, simplifying recurring tasks and enabling new analytical approaches.

03

Methodical discipline

An approach gains substance through careful testing. This requires reliable sources, explicit criteria and a willingness to revise assumptions in light of new evidence.
