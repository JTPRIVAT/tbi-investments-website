The perspective

Technology in context.
Connecting perspectives.

Digital technology brings different disciplines together. Computer science describes how systems are built; data analysis examines observations; an economic perspective considers use and market mechanisms. Where these disciplines meet, questions arise that call for more than one perspective.

An idea's technical feasibility, its practical usefulness and what it can tell us when making decisions are separate questions. Connecting them requires precise concepts and an awareness of what an investigation can establish and what remains open.

Principles

Think clearly.
Examine the possibilities.

01

Analytical clarity

Clear definitions and understandable connections help make sense of complex questions. The distinction between an observation, an assumption and the conclusion drawn from them is essential.

02

Technological curiosity

Openness to new technologies goes hand in hand with an interest in how they work. Their specific purpose and the conditions they require deserve as much attention as the idea itself.

03

Responsible assessment

Possibilities can be assessed carefully when limitations and dependencies are also visible. The scope of a claim should reflect what the available information can actually establish.
