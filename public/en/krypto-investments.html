Area 01 · Crypto

Digital assets.
Understand the connections.

Crypto assets bring together software, digital networks and markets. Understanding them starts with straightforward questions: What does a token represent, what is it used for and how does it relate to its network?

Explore the topics ↗
ASSETS · NETWORKS · APPLICATIONS

Foundations

Every price
has a wider context.

A blockchain can record transactions in a shared ledger. How that ledger is updated, and who can take part, depends on the system. These differences shape what a network can do and the technical limitations it faces.

A token can serve different purposes within such a system, such as enabling access to an application or being used to pay for network activity. Understanding it involves looking at both its technical design and its actual use.

Three perspectives

Technology, use
and the market.

01

Technical foundations

How are transactions confirmed? Who keeps the network running? These questions help explain how a system works and what it depends on.

02

Practical use

What purpose does a token serve within an application? Who needs it, and for what? Describing its use in concrete terms makes the connection between digital infrastructure and potential benefits easier to understand.

03

Markets and prices

Supply, demand and expectations are among the factors that interact in a market. Price and technical function answer different questions. Both perspectives contribute to a considered understanding of the subject.
