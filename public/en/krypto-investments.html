Market dynamics

Analyse price movements.
Identify the context.

Price developments, trading volume and available liquidity are central to analysing crypto markets. Together, they provide insight into changes in buying and selling interest. A clearly defined time horizon helps distinguish short-term movements from longer-term developments.

Market opportunities need to be assessed in context: What is moving the price, how readily can an asset be traded and what costs are involved? Network use and changes in the economic environment also matter. A sharp price movement alone does not answer these questions.

Three perspectives

Dynamics, liquidity
and economic context.

01

Market dynamics

Volatility describes the magnitude of price fluctuations. Their pattern, speed and trading volume help put movements in context and frame specific questions for further analysis.

02

Liquidity

Available buy and sell orders influence the prices at which trading is possible. Liquidity, fees and potential differences between expected and execution prices therefore matter when assessing a market opportunity.

03

Network and use

Actual use, token supply and network development complement price analysis. They help identify the economic assumptions underlying a valuation.
